name: listener-bindtodevice
about: A socket bound to one interface (SO_BINDTODEVICE, ss shows addr%dev) only takes packets
about: that arrived on it, even when they are sent to that interface's own address.

host tcp 0.0.0.0 9000 dev=eth1
host udp 0.0.0.0 9001 dev=eth1
host tcp 0.0.0.0 9002 dev=eth0

name: dnat-to-loopback-no-route-localnet
about: DNAT to 127.0.0.1 from outside: without route_localnet the kernel treats the rewritten
about: packet as a martian and drops it silently, though the filter accepts and a program listens.
--- iptables
*nat
:PREROUTING ACCEPT [0:0]
:INPUT ACCEPT [0:0]
:OUTPUT ACCEPT [0:0]
:POSTROUTING ACCEPT [0:0]
-A PREROUTING -p tcp -m tcp --dport 5432 -j DNAT --to-destination 127.0.0.1:5432
COMMIT
--- listen
host tcp 127.0.0.1 5432
--- probes
eth0 198.51.100.7 203.0.113.10 tcp 5432 want:silent
eth1 192.0.2.50 10.20.0.1 tcp 5432 want:silent

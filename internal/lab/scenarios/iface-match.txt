name: iface-match
about: -i matches the interface a packet arrived on. Accepting eth1 says nothing about eth0,
about: even for a packet sent to eth1's own address.
--- iptables
*filter
:INPUT DROP [0:0]
:FORWARD DROP [0:0]
:OUTPUT ACCEPT [0:0]
-A INPUT -i eth1 -j ACCEPT
COMMIT
--- listen
host tcp 0.0.0.0 22
--- probes
eth1 192.0.2.50 10.20.0.1 tcp 22 want:open
eth1 192.0.2.50 10.20.0.1 tcp 23 want:refused
eth0 198.51.100.7 203.0.113.10 tcp 22 want:silent
eth0 198.51.100.7 10.20.0.1 tcp 22 want:silent
